In 2025, EFRAG has been requested by Commissioner Albuquerque for its technical advice to modify the delegated act on European Sustainability Reporting Standards (ESRS).

EFRAG is expected to deliver its technical advice to the European Commission in the beginning of December 2025.

This project sets out EFRAG's work in this area. You can also find the key latest news on the Simplified ESRS webpage

### 📣 EFRAG Outreach Events on the Revised ESRS Exposure Drafts

EFRAG’s public outreach events took place in September 2025, bringing together stakeholders to provide feedback on the Revised ESRS Exposure Drafts. These sessions complemented the public consultation survey, which remained open until 29 September 2025.

During the events, EFRAG experts presented the main amendments to the revised ESRS and gathered perspectives from national standard setters, business associations, financial institutions, investors and users, and civil society organisations. The outreach series is now concluded, and all registration and programme information has closed.

The full list of events, including recordings and presentations for some events is available: Outreach events

### 📋 Recently Closed EFRAG Field Tests and Consultation Activities

Field Test on Adequate Wage Methodology

EFRAG has completed its targeted field test on the revised methodology for calculating adequate wages for non-EU employees under ESRS S1. The working sessions and questionnaire, conducted between 8 and 26 September 2025, focused on evaluating the clarity and practical feasibility of the new non-EU wage hierarchy. The call for participants closed on 18 August 2025.

Field Test on Gross vs. Net in the DMA

The field test on the updated guidance for considering remediation, mitigation, and prevention actions in the Double Materiality Assessment has concluded. Participating companies joined September 2025 sessions to examine how the “Gross vs. Net” revisions may influence the DMA process. Registration for this test also closed on 18 August 2025.

Cost–Benefit Analysis for ESRS Simplification

EFRAG’s external cost-benefit analysis supporting the ESRS simplification process is now closed. Stakeholders were invited to provide input through an online survey designed to gather insights on the expected impacts of the proposed simplifications. The deadline for submissions passed on 12 September 2025. You can check the survey structure here.
